# 10 · Go-to-Market Plan

## Strategy

**Founder-led, design-partner-first, rejection-intelligence wedge into regulated Indian medical-device plants. Prove ROI on site 1, productize onboarding, repeat. No PLG, no paid ads in year 1.**

## Positioning in market

Category entry: “Trustworthy rejection ledger + money + audit” — not “AI MES.”

## Phases

### Phase 0 — Foundations (months 0–3)
- Freeze commercial pack (this folder).  
- MSA + Pilot SOW with counsel.  
- Close P0 product gaps: auth story, cost governance, deploy path.  
- Design partner commercial path (Disposafe-class).  
- One-pager + deck + demo script polished.  

### Phase 1 — First revenue (months 3–9)
- 1–2 pilots with written success reports.  
- ≥1 annual Plant contract.  
- One real on-prem or production-like install.  
- Support channel live.  

### Phase 2 — Repeatability (months 9–18)
- Second/third plant without heroics.  
- Median impl ≤ 4 weeks.  
- Case study (named or anonymized).  
- Light content (audit readiness, COPQ, Excel failure modes).  
- Optional consultant intros — not exclusive resellers yet.

## Funnel

```
Intro / NDA
  → Problem interview (30–45m)
  → Sample workbook review
  → Live demo (their data or lookalike)
  → Pilot proposal (success criteria + ₹1.25L)
  → 8-week pilot
  → Business review + ROI letter
  → Annual contract (Plant + impl credit)
```

**Cycle time:** 4–16 weeks typical.  
**Concurrent pilots:** max 2 without support hire.

## Anti-GTM

- Free forever for logos without rights.  
- Pilots without kill criteria.  
- Selling OEE before rejection trust exists.  
- Discount wars below floor.
