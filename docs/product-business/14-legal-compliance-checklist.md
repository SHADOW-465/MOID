## Draft commercial principles (for counsel to formalize)

### License grant
Non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use MOID software for internal operations at the named plant site(s), for the subscription term, in object/deployed form only.

### Restrictions
No reverse engineering except to the extent mandatory law; no resale; no use as a service bureau for third-party plants without multi-site license; no removal of proprietary notices.

### Customer data
Customer owns all plant event data and uploaded files. Vendor processes only to provide the service/support. On termination: export assistance and deletion within agreed days (e.g. 30) upon written request.

### Intellectual property
Vendor owns MOID software, ontology engine, and pre-existing IP. Customer owns their data and their confidential process information. Feedback may be used to improve product without identifying customer unless agreed.

### AI annex (draft)
The Software may use ML models to assist with **structure classification** and **natural-language explanation**. **All quantitative metrics are computed by deterministic application code** over stored events. Model outputs are assistive, not authoritative measurements. Optional cloud model features, if enabled by Customer, transmit only content permitted by the Security Exhibit (including de-identification where applicable). Default plant mode may disable egress entirely.

### Warranties (direction)
Software substantially conforms to documentation during term; remedy is re-performance or termination refund of prepaid unused fees (counsel to set caps). No warranty of uninterrupted operation or of achieving specific scrap reduction.

### Liability (direction)
Cap at fees paid in prior 12 months; exclude indirect damages; carve-outs for IP infringement, confidentiality breach, willful misconduct as counsel advises. No unlimited liability for “failed audit” outcomes.

### Audit / compliance claims
Marketing and contract language must **not** guarantee ISO/CDSCO certification or regulatory approval. Say “supports audit readiness” and “ALCOA+-oriented controls.”

## Safe vs risky claims

| Safe | Risky |
|------|-------|
| Supports audit preparation | Guarantees zero audit findings |
| Deterministic calculations | AI-validated yield |
| Helps document CAPA with evidence | Replaces entire QMS for regulatory purposes |
| Reduces egress risk on-prem | Hacker-proof / unbreakable |

## Case study clause (minimum)

Customer grants limited right to use name/logo and a mutually approved description of results; Customer may require anonymization; approval not unreasonably withheld; no confidential process parameters disclosed.
