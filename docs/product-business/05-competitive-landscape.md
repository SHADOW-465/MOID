# 05 · Competitive Landscape

## Alternatives customers actually use

| Alternative | Wins when | Loses when | MOID counter |
|-------------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| **Excel + drives** | Budget zero, habits deep | Multi-user truth, audit, money | Ledger + provenance + roles |
| **Power BI / Tableau** | Clean warehouse exists | Messy multi-file Excel is the source | MOD → events → trusted metrics |
| **Full MES** | Huge budget, full OT program | MSME cost/time | Wedge in weeks–months, not years |
| **eQMS / CAPA docs** | Document control focus | Live rejection economics weak | Live scrap + COPQ + source |
| **Minitab / SPC-only** | Stats depth | Not operational cockpit | SPC inside daily ops + entry |
| **“AI quality” startups** | Hype narrative | Opaque maths, plant distrust | Deterministic KPIs invariant |

## Win / lose themes

### You win when
- Buyer feels Excel pain weekly.  
- Audit or COPQ is on the GM agenda.  
- IT will allow a plant server or controlled pilot.  
- Champion wants evidence, not only charts.

### You lose when
- Deal is “full digital factory” RFP only.  
- Data cannot be shared even under pilot NDA.  
- Buyer wants prediction theater more than baseline truth.  
- Price is forced under floor with no strategic trade.

## Competitive talk tracks

**vs Excel:** “Free tools are expensive when scrap and audits are real. MOID keeps Excel as history input, not as the system of truth.”

**vs BI:** “BI visualizes warehouses. You don’t have a warehouse — you have conflicting workbooks. We build the ledger first.”

**vs MES:** “Respect MES for long-horizon OT. Hire MOID for rejection intelligence this half without a crore-scale program.”

**vs AI vendors:** “If the model can invent a yield, it can invent a lie. Ours is forbidden from maths.”
