# 04 · Positioning & Messaging

## Positioning statement

For **plant GMs and quality leaders at regulated manufacturers** who **bleed money and audit time into untrusted Excel rejection data**, **MOID** is a **rejection-intelligence cockpit** that **creates a trustworthy ledger, money view, and audit trail from their existing sheets and entry habits**. Unlike **Excel, heavy MES, or black-box AI analytics**, MOID **never lets the model invent numbers** — every KPI is deterministic and source-traceable.

## Category language

| Prefer | Avoid |

| Rejection intelligence | Full MES |
| Quality cockpit | Revolutionary AI platform |
| Audit-ready ledger | Predictive black box |

| Cost of poor quality | Vanity dashboard |

## Messaging house

### Pillar 1 — Trust
- Your rejection % is a claim until it’s a ledger.  
- AI that never does the maths — so you can trust the maths.  
- Every major number has View Source.

### Pillar 2 — Money
- See scrap in rupees, not just pieces.  
- Progressive cost of poor quality by stage.  
- Fund software from scrap you stop losing.

### Pillar 3 — Audit
- ALCOA+-oriented trail without weeks of folder digging.  
- One-click audit package with hash manifest.  
- Findings, not silent spreadsheet fixes.

### Pillar 4 — Fit
- Your column names. Your stages. Your defects.  
- Excel for history; Data Entry for every day after.  
- On-prem first for plants that cannot leak data.

## Elevator pitches

### 15 seconds
MOID makes plant rejection data trustworthy — ledger, rupees, and audit trail — without buying a MES or trusting Excel formulas.

### 30 seconds
Medical-device plants run quality on Excel. Numbers fight, audits panic, scrap is invisible as money. MOID maps sheets once, runs daily entry in plant language, and computes every KPI in code with full provenance. AI only helps classify and explain — it never invents a yield.

### 60 seconds
Regulated plants already capture rejection in weekly and monthly workbooks, but formulas break, files overlap, and the GM still cannot see which stage, defect, or size is burning cash. MOID snapshots Excel, verifies column meaning with humans, commits events to an append-only ledger, and powers a factory cockpit — dashboard, drills, SPC, COPQ, and audit export. We deploy on the plant LAN when security requires it. In an 8-week pilot we baseline your numbers, prove entry, and show payback against scrap and engineer time. You buy an annual plant license when the success criteria clear.

## Tone of voice

- Calm, industrial, specific — Linear/Stripe seriousness, not startup hype.  
- Prefer numbers and mechanisms over adjectives.  
- Honest about findings and method differences.  
- Never claim certification or guaranteed scrap reduction.

## Banned phrases (customer-facing)

- “AI-powered yield optimization engine”  
- “Replace your entire quality system tomorrow”  
- “Guaranteed 25% scrap cut”  
- “Fully autonomous plant brain”  
- “We fix your Excel automatically without human checks”

## Proof points (use when true)

- Deterministic analytics over append-only ledger  
- Provenance / View Source  
- Clean-month parity goal with client ground truth  
- Doubling-stability of KPIs under re-ingest  
- Audit ZIP + SHA-256 manifest  
- Design partner domain depth (medical device multi-stage)
