# 16 · Twelve-Month Operating Plan

## North star (month 12)

- **≥3 plants** live **or** 1 flagship design partner + **2 paid** plants  
- Repeatable onboarding (median ≤ 4 weeks impl)  
- Price book used without improvisation  
- ≥1 case study (named or anonymized)  
- On-prem story proven at least once  
- P0 product gaps closed or contractually contained  

## Quarterly plan

### Q1 — Foundations
**Outcomes:** Legal v1, price freeze, design-partner path, P0 eng started, sales assets designed.  
**Exit:** Can run a paid pilot SOW without inventing terms.

Checklist:
- [ ] Counsel: Pilot + MSA + Order Form  
- [ ] One-pager + deck designed  
- [ ] Auth MVP plan executing  
- [ ] Cost config governance plan executing  
- [ ] 20-account outreach list  
- [ ] Design partner commercial conversation  

### Q2 — First revenue
**Outcomes:** 1–2 pilots completed; ≥1 annual contract; support process live.  
**Exit:** Money in bank; ROI letter template used for real.

Checklist:
- [ ] Pilot #1 success report  
- [ ] First `MOID-PLANT-Y1` signed  
- [ ] Install runbook used once  
- [ ] Severity support log started  

### Q3 — Repeatability
**Outcomes:** Second paid plant; impl time down; case study draft.  
**Exit:** Second install doesn’t require rewrite.

Checklist:
- [ ] Plant #2 live  
- [ ] Impl playbook v2  
- [ ] Case study approved or anonymized  
- [ ] Multi-site inquiry handled with price book  

### Q4 — Scale readiness
**Outcomes:** P0 closed; decide year-2 hosted SKU; capacity plan.  
**Exit:** Honest “we are a product company” checklist green.

Checklist:
- [ ] P0 gaps done or dated  
- [ ] Hire trigger review  
- [ ] Year-2 plan written  
- [ ] Retro of pricing (raise/lower?)  

## Time allocation (solo default)

| Activity | Share |
|----------|-------|
| Product engineering | 50% |
| Pilot / customer success | 30% |
| Sales / packaging | 15% |
| Legal / admin | 5% |

**If overloaded:** stop non-wedge features; finish trust; run fewer concurrent pilots.

## Hiring triggers

| Trigger | First hire shape |
|---------|------------------|
| 2+ plants + support pain | Part-time support/impl |
| Full pipeline, founder bottleneck | BD/sales assist |
| Heavy on-prem demand | DevOps contractor |

## Budget categories (order-of-magnitude)

- Legal templates  
- Minimal web presence  
- Travel for critical installs  
- Optional local GPU for demos  
- **No** large ad budget year 1  

## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Free pilot forever | Med | High | Calendar + fee + credit rules |
| Snowflake custom each plant | High | High | Package scope; MOD discipline |
| IT blocks cloud | High | Med | Lead on-prem |
| Number method fights | High | High | Calculation policy + dual display |
| Founder burnout | Med | High | Max 2 pilots; severity policy |
| Design partner doesn’t pay | Med | Med | Paid pilot; case rights trade only with value |

## Definition: “we are a product company”

All true:
1. Stranger plant scoped in one workshop.  
2. Contract + price book exist and are used.  
3. Install doesn’t rewrite the engine.  
4. Support is severity-based, not only WhatsApp chaos.  
5. Second customer doesn’t require a greenfield rewrite.  

## Monthly operating cadence

- Week 1: pipeline + pilot status  
- Week 2: product P0 review  
- Week 3: customer success / support load  
- Week 4: commercial metrics (ACV, conversion, cash)
